# The Pixel That Connects Your Site to Search Atlas

The OTTO Pixel is a single script that connects your website to Search Atlas. Once it's live, OTTO SEO reads your pages, fixes what's holding your rankings back, and deploys the changes straight to your site, on its own, across any CMS.

## Connect the Pixel. OTTO Takes It From There.

The pixel is the connection. Behind it, OTTO SEO reads your site against Google's ranking factors and fixes what it finds: meta tags, entity coverage, on-page content, and more, without you touching the code.

### Technical SEO Fixes

OTTO finds the technical issues holding back your visibility and fixes them on its own, no developer ticket required.

### Schema Markup

OTTO adds the schema your pages are missing, FAQ, product, and more, so they stand out further in the search results.

### Google Business Profile

OTTO manages your Google Business Profile on its own: posts, review replies, Q&A, and the fixes that lift local rankings.

### Meta Tag Best Practices

OTTO writes and ships meta tags across every page, keeping keywords in and character counts in range, without you touching a template.

### Content Creation

OTTO writes and publishes original content straight to your site on its own, building the pages that bring in more organic traffic.

### Content Optimization

OTTO adds the entities and semantic terms your pages are missing, closing the topical gaps between you and page one.

## Connect the OTTO Pixel to Your Site

To connect Search Atlas to your site, add the OTTO Pixel to your website header. One paste, and OTTO SEO can start reading your pages and shipping fixes.

### Where To Find Your OTTO Pixel

Inside your dashboard, click on OTTO & Audit in the left navigation, click on the +Create button and copy your pixel code.

### Add the above pixel to your website header

Once it's live, OTTO SEO starts auditing your site data on its own and lines up the fixes it's ready to make.

### Deploy on autopilot, or review first

By default, OTTO SEO deploys fixes on its own. Want to sign off first? Turn on approval mode to review every change before it goes live, and roll back anything, anytime.
