# LinkedIn public profile visibility | LinkedIn Help
URL: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a518980
Last updated: 10 months ago (relative to fetch)

## Manage your public profile information

Control what sections of a profile are eligible for display to people who are not signed in to LinkedIn, or can be viewed on search tools such as Google or Bing.

## Important to know

After changes are made to a public profile, it can take several weeks or months at times for search tools like Google, Yahoo, or Bing to detect changes and refresh. LinkedIn doesn't control that refresh process.

A LinkedIn public profile is a simplified version of the full LinkedIn profile. Not all sections of a profile can be displayed publicly. On the Public profile settings page, a member can see and adjust which sections of their profile are eligible to be displayed publicly. In some contexts LinkedIn may show an even more limited profile preview that includes some but not all of the profile fields a member has allowed to be public.

A public profile may be visible to:
- People who aren't LinkedIn members
- Viewers who aren't signed in to LinkedIn
- Those who haven't linked their LinkedIn account to their account on other approved services, subject to off-LinkedIn visibility settings

The public profile appears:
- When people search for a member using search tools like Google, Yahoo!, Bing, DuckDuckGo, etc.
- On public profile badges
- On affiliate and approved other services like Outlook

## Notes (verbatim list from the source, most directly relevant to an unauthenticated audit)

- The default photo setting is Public.
- If a member edits the visibility settings of their profile photo from their profile page, the public profile page updates with the new setting (e.g. changing profile photo visibility from Public to "1st-degree connections only" means only 1st-degree connections see it going forward).
- Photo visibility settings can be updated while editing the public profile page, or by disabling the public profile entirely.
- **"In order to see your full profile, visitors may need to log in to their LinkedIn accounts."** This is the source's own explicit statement that the logged-out public profile view is a stripped, incomplete version of the full profile, not the full profile itself.
- The Articles & Activity setting toggle is not present if the member hasn't made any post.
- Disabling the public profile hides it from non-LinkedIn members and from search-tool results. Disabling it could also prevent account recovery if the member loses access to their primary email address, since LinkedIn may require the public profile URL for that.
- LinkedIn does not share personal data with any other external platform without consent.

## Practical read for an unauthenticated audit

Because sections are individually togglable by the profile owner (photo, headline, summary, experience, skills, articles/activity, and more, per the source's related "Control your public LinkedIn profile" article), what an auditor without a login sees on any given LinkedIn profile (personal or, by extension, the analogous logged-out view of a Company Page) is not a fixed, guaranteed set of fields; it is whatever subset that specific account owner has left toggled to public, which the source states can be a genuinely limited preview. This is a structural limitation on any unauthenticated LinkedIn audit, not a bug or an inconsistency to flag as a finding.
